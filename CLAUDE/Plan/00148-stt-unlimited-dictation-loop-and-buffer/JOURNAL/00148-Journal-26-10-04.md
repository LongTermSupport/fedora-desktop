## 21:04 · decision · P9   — paste where the owner is looking

Phase 9 filed and built, from the owner's reports after two long dictations into GNOME
Text Editor that transcribed in full and never pasted.

**Cause, from `debug.log`:** 4525 chars transcribed, clipboard verified, `Ctrl+Shift+V`
sent, then Enter. The editor (`org.gnome.TextEditor`, GTK 4) has no `Ctrl+Shift+V`
binding. Pasting into kitty worked throughout. The panel's line naming the window was
lost because `wsi-stream` emptied the shared `debug.log` at each start.

**Decisions:**

- Terminals are recognised by their desktop entry's `TerminalEmulator` category
  (verified: kitty and Ptyxis have it, Text Editor does not). No list of terminals is
  kept by hand.
- The owner asked whether a paste can be verified, or both keys tried. Neither:
  ydotool reports nothing back, and accessibility readback is blind in terminals and
  Electron apps. `Ctrl+V` in a terminal is quoted-insert, and a browser would paste twice.
- The paste key is decided in one place, the panel's `_pasteTargetForFocus`. It serves
  both the key chosen at Insert and the `PasteKey` D-Bus call `wsi-stream` makes before
  each paste. A panel without `PasteKey` (until the owner's next login) leaves the key
  chosen at Insert, with a logged warning.
- Chunked pasting (`dictation-paste-interval-seconds`, default 0) uses the server's
  `text_so_far`, which only grows: `Transcript.text` joins committed phrases and none is
  revised. The owner chose 120 s chunks that follow focus, with Enter only at stop. The
  owner was concerned that chunking would stop Whisper revising earlier text; it does
  not, because each VAD phrase is transcribed once and earlier text is only a prompt.
- `Ctrl+S` after paste is opt-in per app (`paste-save-apps`) and never sent to a terminal
  (XOFF).
- Boundary (owner): Phase 9 is the last injection work built here. Before IBus, voice
  commands or a document mode, the owner tries Vocalinux. Survey:
  `research-linux-dictation-landscape.md`.

Not tested by a unit test: `_pasteTargetForFocus` and `focusOutline.js`. The GJS stubs
have no `Shell`, `Meta` or window objects. Covered by the host check, Task 9.5.

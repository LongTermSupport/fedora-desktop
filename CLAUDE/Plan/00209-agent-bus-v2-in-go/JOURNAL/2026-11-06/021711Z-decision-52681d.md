## 02:17 · decision · —   — The retry hold already lifts; the slow delivery is the up-mark

The retry hold (fix 2, `fix/retry-hold-lift`): no production change. A newer answered sync
already lifts a standing hold without waiting `retryHold`, a sync answered before the failure
does not, and the timer fires with none: pinned by `TestN3dD1ANewerSyncLiftsTheRetryHold`
(green, three plants red). The E2E's 55 to 62 s delivery is the team's up-mark: a team back
after an outage resumes from its stored position with no sync of its own and is shown up only
when its first long-poll answers, `longPoll` after the retry in an idle room (measured on the
fake clock; pinned by `TestABackTeamIsShownUpAtItsFirstLongPoll`). The wiring bounds now derive
from that (`upBound = backoffCap + longPoll + settle`, `deliveredBound = upBound`), and
`TestE2EOfflineServer`'s up wait moves from `backoffCap + settle` to `upBound`. Whether a team
should be marked up at its first answered request is left to the coordinator; the evidence and
the recommendation are in `subagent-reports/261105-fix-sender-opus-report.md`.

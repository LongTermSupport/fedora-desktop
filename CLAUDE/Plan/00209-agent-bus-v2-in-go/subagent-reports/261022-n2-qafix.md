## Decisions

- **D1.** The take-side check reads the member-writable session directory. That is accepted
  because a refusal there stops only that session's own take, before anything is minted, and
  revokes nothing (n2c-D14 is about revocation).
- **D2.** Ownership is decided by `since_ms`, made strictly increasing per session and team under
  the allocation lock. A tie or a backward clock step would otherwise move a live copy or strand
  one. Strict ordering covers both, so the comparison stays `>=`.
- **D3.** The release's scan of the team's pool is fail-fast. A strange name or an unreadable
  sibling `held/` stops the release before step 4, naming the entry. The pool is the owner's
  alone, so no member can cause this. The test pins it.
- **D4.** The refusal keeps `ErrHeld`, as the task asked, and widens its doc: seat choice must
  not answer it by trying the next seat.
- **D5.** The step-1 judgement outside the lock only spares a wait; the move is decided under the
  lock.

## Design-text fix proposed

C 3.7.3 should say who owns `sessions/<session>/<team>` when two holdings name one session. The
answer is the rule of the n2c-D12 addendum: one copy per session and team, owned by the newest
holding.

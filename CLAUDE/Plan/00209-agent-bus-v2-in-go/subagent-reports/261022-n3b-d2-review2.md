## What holds (recomputed)

- **Lengths.** The unit is 17 + 22 + 1 + 24 + 1 + 4 + 1 + 16 + 8 = 94 bytes, under 255. The team
  is at most 22 (A 2.2) and the seat at most 4 digits (1-9999). The tmux session is 86 bytes,
  all in `[a-z0-9_-]`, so it holds no `.` or `:`. `/run/teamchat-session-<key>` is 38 bytes.
  `link.sock` is 38 + 14 + 16 + 10 = 78 bytes, `cc-socks/<pid>.sock` at most 60 bytes (pid_max
  4194304, 7 digits), and `tmux.sock` 48 bytes. B places no other socket. A hand-run seat
  under `/run/user/<uid>` (uid up to 10 digits) gives a `link.sock` of at most 70 bytes. All are
  under 107. `validCheckout` (declare/values.go:86) makes the checkout clean and absolute, so
  the key's input is canonical, and `<team>\n<checkout>\n<seat>` is unambiguous: team and seat
  can hold no newline.
- **Every name is derivable** from the entry, or for a record-only leaver from the record entry
  (container, checkout, seat, user). That holds for a stopped container and before the first
  join.
- **One coordinator per team** comes through DC6's `coordinator`, and no entry has a `role` key.
- **The `--key` launch rule** agrees across B (path table, 2.2.1 row, 2.4.1's opening), CL5, C
  3.9.4, B 6 C3 and D 1212. "Every rule for a given `--desired` holds for the derived
  directory" keeps n3-D16's 78 and the inbound rule.
- **The absent phase does not re-park a `left` entry.** The WIP code checks this
  (absent.go:100, `prior.Status != StatusLeft`). F-B depends on it.

## Findings

### F-A. MAJOR: step 06's "the child records `retired`" contradicts n2d-D21 and the built child, so step 03 never sees the retired handle

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1801-1805` (step 06), `SPEC-n3.md:320-323` (LX7), `SPEC-n2d.md:66-67`
(MO4) and `:154` (n2d-D21), rework `:123-125` and `:177`.

Failure: n2d-D21 says "An explicit seat is refused with no write". The child does exactly that:
`apply/member.go:190` records `retired` only when `req.Seat == 0` (`--seat next`); an explicit
seat returns the refusal and writes nothing. Since n3b-D2 every `lxc apply` add is explicit. So a
seat whose account is deactivated is never recorded `retired`, the next apply's step 03 finds no
retired handle, and step 06 meets the same refusal again. The entry is `DEFERRED` on every apply,
and the "refused by name, choose another seat" line that C and LX7 promise never comes. The phase
does not fail, but the owner is never told to change the seat. The rework file lists no child
change, and its "Unaffected" line (177) says the child's verbs change only for `--seat next`.
`lxc apply` also has no stated way to tell a deactivated-account refusal from any other refusal
of `add`.

Fix: choose one and write it into MO4, n2d-D21, C step 06, LX7 and the rework file.

- (a) Explicit `add --seat S` whose account is deactivated records `member <h> retired`, then
  refuses with a distinct refusal code. This is a rework item in `apply/member.go`'s `admit`, with
  a test. `lxc apply` maps that code, and only that code, to the entry's `DEFERRED`.
- (b) Keep the child as it is. Say that step 06's DEFERRED repeats on every apply, naming
  "choose another seat", and drop "the next apply's step 03 refuses it by name".

(a) matches what C promises.

The same step should also cover `return`. `SPEC-n2d.md:62` refuses a `return` of a deactivated
account, and the user move and the container move both join by `return` (MINOR on its own).

### F-B. MAJOR: a seat moved to another container while the old one is stopped gives the record two entries with one handle, which the record refuses, so the present phase fails

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1760-1765` (step 03's "not `left`"), `:1872-1875` (absent step 5),
`:1885-1888` (stopped container), `:1930-1932` and `:1621-1622` ("one per handle", "maps each
handle to one entry"), `SPEC-n3.md:337-343` (LX8) and `:350-351` (LX9), `:373` (LP1). The built
check is `lxcstate.go:193-204` ("two entries hold one handle", any status), with `ByHandle` at
`:146`.

Failure: the owner moves seat 3 of `/home/u/proj` from container A, which is stopped, to
container B, at the same seat.

1. The absent phase parks handle H from the host. A's record entry stays `left`, files pending.
2. The present phase derives H for B's entry. F4's rule leaves `left` out of the equal-handle
   check, the roster says `parked`, so B joins by `member return`.
3. The record now holds (A, …, 3, `left`, H) and (B, …, 3, `joined`, H).

`Record.check` refuses that, so step 10's write fails after the roster change. Every play then
fails while A stays stopped: a stopped container makes the phase fail, which n3b-D1 forbids. The
same happens when A is running but its seat is still held after absent step 4 (`left` with
`why`).

Holding B back until A is cleaned would instead make a stopped container block another seat,
which is also forbidden. So the record has to admit the pair. Even without the encoder,
LP1/`ByHandle` ("(team, handle) to the record entry") becomes ambiguous and may probe A's dead
seat for B's online state.

Fix:

- 3.9.3 and LX9: unique by handle **among entries not `left`**; a `left` entry may share its
  handle with one live entry.
- LP1 and 3.9.2's L3 paragraph map a handle to the entry that is not `left`.
- State in the stopped-container paragraph that a `left` entry is never parked again, which the
  code already does. Otherwise a later absent phase would park B's live member.
- Rework: `lxcstate` `Record.check` and `ByHandle` change. The current text says "it already
  enforces one per handle" (rework:67), as though nothing changes.
- LX8 tests: a move whose old container is stopped (record written, B joined, A cleaned at its
  next start, B untouched), and one whose old seat is still held.

### F-C. MAJOR: the rework file still briefs the unit's `ExecStart` with `--state`/`--desired`

Where: rework `:79-81`.

Failure: it says `ExecStart` gets "`--state`/`--desired` from `paths.LXCLayout`" and that the CL5
parse test "keeps passing (`--state` with `--desired` is allowed)". C 3.9.4's `ExecStart`
(C:1989) and CL5 now say `--key <key>`, and `--key` with `--state` or `--desired` is 64. A builder
briefed from this file renders a unit whose line the new parser refuses, or keeps `--state` and
`--desired` legal for kind `lxc`.

Fix: "`ExecStart` with `tmux -S <socket> new-session -d -s <unit stem>` and `teamchat run --claude --kind lxc --key <key> --resume-recorded -- claude`; the CL5 parse test of this line
follows the `--key` rule".

### F-D. MAJOR: SPEC-n2b's declaration and approval-gate rows are still per user

Where: `SPEC-n2b.md:65` (DR: "No two entries share a team, container and user"), `:113` (AG1:
LXC entries "as team, container, user and `human_text`"), `:116` (AG2's `lxc_members` schema
without `checkout`/`seat`), `:123` (AG3's entry text `<container>/<user>,human_text=<bool>`),
`:136` and `:141` (AG6's `--set` "replaces the entry with that container and user", and its entry
grammar).

Failure: C 3.5.1 (C:639) now gates checkout and seat, and the rework (`:32-42`) changes
`readLXC` and `gate.go`. But the spec that owns those packages still states the old key, the old
`approved.json` shape and the old `--add`/`--remove`/`--set` entry text. The n3b-D2 row's
"Amends" list (SPEC-n3:914) omits SPEC-n2b. Two seats of one user in one container are one
approve entry under AG3's text, and AG6's `--set` cannot name a seat. The new entry text also
needs a grammar: a checkout may contain `,` or `=` (`validCheckout` allows both), so
`<container>/<checkout>,seat=…,human_text=…` is ambiguous unless it is quoted or JSON-escaped.

Fix:

- Amend DR, AG1, AG2, AG3 and AG6 in SPEC-n2b with a pointer to n3b-D2.
- Give AG3 an entry text that cannot be ambiguous. For example `<container>/<seat>:<checkout>`
  with the checkout last, or a JSON-quoted checkout. Make `user` a field of the entry, not part
  of its name, so a user move is a `--set`.
- Add SPEC-n2b to the n3b-D2 row's "Amends" list, and add the AG3 grammar to the rework's
  `gate.go` item.

### F-E. MINOR: the user move holds the new user back only "while the container is stopped", but a held seat leaves the same pending state, and the retry would then remove the new user's unit

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1723-1725` and `:1849-1851` ("(a stopped container)"). LX8
(`SPEC-n3.md:340-341`) says it more generally.

Failure: absent step 4 times out for the old user. The entry is kept `left` with "seat still held
…", and steps 2-5 run again next apply. Step 01 says the present phase is not refused and joins
the new user by `return`. The unit name, the runtime directory and the host copy
`_lxc/<container>/<key>.json` contain no user, so the new user's seat shares them with the old
one. The next apply's retry of step 3 then stops and removes the new user's unit, and its step 2
rewrites the shared host copy with a `farewell`.

Fix: in both places, replace "(a stopped container)" with "while the record entry for the key
stays `left` under the old user (a stopped container, or a seat still held)". Add the held case to
LX8's user-move tests.

### F-F. MINOR: a held-back entry's record line can overwrite a `left` entry

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1912-1915` (3.9.3: a present entry with a known handle on a stopped
container is `SKIPPED`, status `offline`) and `:1849-1851`.

Failure: in a user move with the container stopped, one key has a `left` record entry under the
old user. The present phase's stopped-container rule would write `offline`, possibly with the new
`user`. Either the old user's pending files are then never cleaned, or the "DEFERRED (files under
its old user …)" line contradicts the `SKIPPED` line.

Fix: one sentence in 3.9.3. A `left` entry is changed only by the absent phase. A held-back
present entry whose key has a `left` entry for another user prints the user-move `DEFERRED` and
leaves the record entry as it is.

### F-G. MINOR: `seat set-aside` has no way to name an LXC seat

Where: B 2.2.1 (`DESIGN-v2-B-member.md:389`), B 2.3.2 (`:718`) and SA1 (`SPEC-n3.md:181`).

Failure: B gives the owner's only repair for an undecodable store as `teamchat seat set-aside --team <team>`. In a guest that acts on the default `~/.config/teamchat/seats`, which no LXC seat
uses any more, and two seats of one user in one team cannot be told apart by `--team`. The
built verb has `--state DIR` (`member/cli/verbs.go:215`), but neither B nor SA1 mentions it.

Fix: either add `--key K` to `seat set-aside` (kind LXC, deriving the state root as `run`
does), or say in B 2.3.2 and SA1 that an LXC seat's repair is `seat set-aside --team T --state ~/.config/teamchat/lxc/<key>/seats`. Add the line to the rework file either way.

### F-I. MINOR: the rework's `LXCLayout` fixes the run root, but a hand-run `service: false` seat runs under `/run/user/<uid>`

Where: rework `:57-58` ("run root under `GuestRuntimeDir(k)`") and `:116-118` (`run` derives
from `paths.LXCLayout`).

Failure: B 2.4.1 step 1 takes `<v2-run>`'s parent from `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`. The unit sets it to
`/run/teamchat-session-<key>`, but the hand-run line of C:2003 gets the login session's
`/run/user/<uid>`. If `run` takes `LXCLayout(home, k)`'s fixed run root, the hand-run seat fails
78 `runtime` (the directory does not exist), or writes outside its runtime directory.

Fix: `LXCLayout(home, runtimeDir string, k SeatKey)`. The state and desired directories come from
the key, and the run root comes from `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, as `HomeLayout` does. The kit scan
already reads both parents (LX5).

### F-J. MINOR: after a user move the handle is derived again, which skips the "origin changed" refusal; the step 03 message is stale

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1756-1759` and `:1872-1875`.

Failure: a completed removal drops the record entry. The new user's join is then a "first join":
the handle is derived again from the checkout, and a changed origin silently becomes `member add`
of a new handle, leaving the old one parked. The same is true of any leave and re-add. Step 03's
message "retire and add the member to change its handle" no longer describes a working route.
With the record keyed by seat, the route is to give the entry another seat; a retired handle at
the same seat is refused by the new retired rule.

Fix:

- Change the message to "the checkout's origin changed; give the entry another seat to take a new
  handle".
- Either keep the old handle through a user move, by carrying it from the dropped record entry to
  the new user's join in the same play, or state that a user move re-derives the handle.

### F-K. MINOR: a derived handle that the roster holds `active` with no record entry is refused only at step 06, after other teams' changes

Where: `DESIGN-v2-C-host.md:1760-1765` and `:908-912`.

Failure: an LXC handle `active` in the roster but in no record entry can follow a restore, where
the record is older than the roster. `member add --seat S` refuses it ("seat number is taken").
That refusal is not the deactivated case, so it fails the phase part-applied after other teams'
demotions and joins.

Fix: step 03 also refuses, by name and before any change, an entry with no record handle whose
derived handle the roster holds in any state other than `parked` or `lost`, alongside the retired
rule. Add a test to LX1.

## Verdict

FIX-FIRST: BLOCK 0, MAJOR 4 (F-A, F-B, F-C, F-D), MINOR 7 (F-E to F-K). The core design holds:
the per-seat names and their lengths, `--key`, the offline rule at the step level, and the
coordinator through DC6. F-B is the one finding that breaks the offline rule: a seat moved away
from a stopped container fails the present phase.

## The exported API of C5

- `Run(ctx, d Deps, held *teamlock.Lock, req Request) (Answer, error)` validates the request,
  then runs steps 03 to 10.
- `Resume(ctx, d, held, team) (Answer, error)` is RS12. It reads the record, the tar and the
  applied team.json and config. It reads no meta and no min_backup.
- `Discard(ctx, d, team) (Answer, error)` is RS13. It takes no lock argument, because the verb
  holds the lock. The wire adapter drops `held`.
- `Answer{Team, Backup, Offline, Pre, Lost, SinceMS, Lists, Notes, Changed, Discarded, Printed}`.
  - `Encode()` is RS9's JSON. A discard encodes as `{"v":1,"team","backup","discarded":true,"notes"}`.
  - `Printed` marks RS8's "restored; clean-up failed" and "status refresh failed". In those
    cases the answer comes beside the error.
- Exit codes:
  - a refusal (`ErrRefused`) is 70. This covers a validation refusal, RS2's plant, a discard
    past staged, and a resume or discard with no record;
  - a failure of the child against the live server wraps `matrix.ErrUnreachable` (69, RS15).
    This covers RS5's export and RS7's live check, online only;
  - everything else is 1.
- **Not defined, as handoff commit 10442cbf0 says:** `ErrServerLost` and `NeedRecord`. The C6
  builder owns `Recorded(root)`, `Records` and `ErrUnproven`. The lead's brief asked for both
  names. A message to the lead names the conflict, and no answer has come.

## Coverage and tests

- The package's coverage is 94.7% under -race.
- golangci-lint reports nothing, with and without `--build-tags tuwunel`.
- The tests are in `engine_test.go` and `engine_kill_test.go`, all over the world.

The tests cover:

- **The whole effect log of the happy path, online and offline.** They check that RS3's verify
  comes before `syncfs`, which comes before `staged`. They check that RS6's
  `write restore-epoch.json` comes before `write restore.json=swap`, and that `fsync` comes
  before `write restore.json=start`. They also check the end state, RS9's answer, the status
  event and the unload wait.
- **SM5's sweep.** It kills at every effect of the online and the offline run (RO7's
  crash-looping unit, which RS18 sees `failed`). After each kill it checks "db absent implies
  block present" and "a restored db with no block implies the record is at start or later".
  Then it resumes, or runs afresh after a resume from staging, until the team is restored.
  The epoch floor ends at 3.
- **A mutation check.** Removing the block before the record is written at start turned the
  sweep and the named start test red. The change was then reverted.
- **The named crash points:**
  - the kill between `write restore.json=start` and `remove restore.block`, after which the
    resume serves;
  - `--roster-from-backup` killed at swap, at start and at clean. min_backup is then 8, and a
    fresh restore of 7 is refused;
  - a kill after the bootstrap wrote the next rev;
  - a kill after the epoch write. The file is 0600, with no temporary beside it, and the resume
    neither raises it again nor takes a second backup.
- **The faults:**
  - the verify answers the backup's epoch;
  - the staging unit restarts, or is new or failed after secure and after check;
  - the staging unit stays loaded past 10 s;
  - a plant appears at `write config.restore.toml`: refused, nothing run, the plant left, and
    RV2 names it next time;
  - the RS10 clean-up fails, the record stays at staging, and the resume cleans up;
  - RS4 or RS5 fails, the record stays staged and the team serves; the resume exports again;
  - a staging unit is active at RS6: refused with no rename, and reverted;
  - the bootstrap fails, the team reverts, the record shows served, and the discard notes it;
  - an offline revert leaves the team down at staged, and nothing is served;
  - a revert's revert or serve fails, the record stays at revert, and the resume fixes it;
  - a stuck revert keeps the record and the block, and the discard removes only the record;
  - `child finish`, `status` or the clean write fails, and the answer is Printed;
  - every effect from RS6 on fails once, and the team recovers;
  - a discard is refused at swap, start and clean;
  - the discard's only-copy note;
  - there is no record, or a bad record, tar or config.

## Decisions (C1-C5)

- **The record.** The record carries `"from_backup"`. Its `lists` hold every list and
  `pending`. `"stuck"` appears only beside `revert`.
- **The merge.** A retired handle's `needs` is cleared. A human found only in the backup is left
  out of the merged roster and locked. RM12 is as C1 built it.
- **StagingArgv** takes the sandbox and the version as data.
- **The FS.** `OS.Remove` only unlinks. `StateOwnerUID` also owns root's own files for
  `ReadOwnedFile`.
- **RS16's refusal** is a `StuckError`, exit 1 and not a refusal. Only `--discard` repairs it.
- **Validation:**
  - RV2 lists ctl's `*.previous` files by their names only, and never opens them;
  - RV7 starts a unit only when it is loaded, `inactive` and has NRestarts 0;
  - RO1 probes 30 times, one second apart, the first at once.
- **Deps** has `RegKey`, `Sandbox`, `Version` and `Self` beyond SM4's list.
- **C5, RS1.** If the staging record write itself fails, the answer is "restore not done", and
  there is nothing to clean up.
- **C5, RS2's plant refusal.** RS10 runs without removing the copy, so the next validation's RV2
  names it. The refusal stays 70 because it is wrapped with `%w`.
- **C5, RS3.** The staging unit must be `active` after each start, and the same run (the same
  InvocationID, NRestarts 0, `active`) after secure and after check. The unload poll reads the
  state up to 11 times, with 10 sleeps of one second.
- **C5, RS4.** The step is skipped when the restore is offline, under `--roster-from-backup`
  (the tar needs the current roster, `noteNoPre`), or when `pre` is already set. A number below
  1 is a failure.
- **C5, RS6 step 2.** A resume at swap writes the block and the record again, which is
  harmless. Any failure after the swap record is reverted as RS11 "not from start". This
  includes the failure of the block's removal after `start`.
- **C5, RS12.** A resume at start whose block removal fails reverts from start, with
  `served: true`.
- **C5, RS7.** A revert from start always sets `served`, even when the start never happened.
  That is the spec's wording, kept.
- **C5, RS8 step 2.** min_backup is one above the highest canonical decimal regular file in
  `meta/`, but never below N + 1. It is written only when that is higher.
- **C5, revert messages.** A failed revert answers "team T is down: restore --resume or
  --discard after fixing <err> (the restore had failed: <cause>)". A stuck revert's message
  names `--discard` and the listing.
- **C5, the answer.** `changed` holds the child's lines (secure, swap, finish, revert) and the
  bootstrap's lines, for this run only, as they were answered.

## Merge glue

- **wire's adapters:**
  - units.Manager to `restore.Units`, mapping A2's state type to `UnitState`;
  - hostnet to `Readiness`;
  - `host.Status`, A3's Refresh with events, to `Status`;
  - `host.Serve`, and `host.BaseSandbox` to `Sandbox`;
  - a host export of the units.json version (`Version`) and of `regKeyOf` (`RegKey`);
  - `backup.Take` behind `Deps.Backup`, and `backup.Decode` with its read of `<n>.tar` behind
    `Deps.Tar`;
  - admin's `restore.Child`, from A4;
  - a `dynamicNames` row wherever admin runs `SecureArgv`'s argv.
- **admin.Restorer** turns `Answer` into `Encode()` bytes, or nil when there is no answer or no
  run happened. It calls `Discard(ctx, d, team)` without `held`.
- **The exit code (C6).** `admin.ExitCodeOf` checks `*ChildError` before
  `matrix.ErrUnreachable`. For RS15's 69 on a lost server, the verb's mapping must check
  `matrix.ErrUnreachable` first, or the engine's wrap is never seen. Choosing between the two
  is a C6 decision.
- **Files.** `child.go` and `child_test.go` match A4's byte for byte (ead39222c), so the merge
  takes either copy.

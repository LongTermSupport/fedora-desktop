## Decisions so far

- **D1. Stop reasons combine.** When several blocks are due, one block carries every reason,
  joined by a space, and every guard field is written in that one write.
- **D2. The failure line is shared, and down teams count for the prompt.** The prompt's
  failure lines include down teams (`TeamDown`) and failed ones. The line repeats every 10
  minutes and shares `failure_ms` with Stop (n3e-D11b). Stop blocks only for failed teams
  (n3e-D12).
- **D3. The repeat times are wall ms.** session.json's times are `optionalMS`. A clock stepped
  back makes a repeat due.
- **D4. End reasons map to the closed set.** `exit` and `prompt_input_exit` record `exit`;
  `logout` records `logout`; anything else records `other`. `clear` and `resume` record
  nothing.
- **D5. Waiting is counted, not listed.** It counts the wait requests running, an atomic in
  `wait`. The engine's `waiters` list keeps stale entries, so it cannot answer this.
- **D6. The pending counts are what the socket will not push.** They are the not-pushed set,
  so the prompt's line and Stop's digest agree.

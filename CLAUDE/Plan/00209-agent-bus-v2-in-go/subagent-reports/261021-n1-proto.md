## What was built

`src-go/internal/teamchat/proto`: area A of the v2 design as pure functions, with no I/O and no
clock (a time is an argument). `doc.go` lists what each file owns:

- the strict JSON decoder and the compact form (A 2.1);

- identifiers and handles (A 2.2, 2.3);

- the roster, its trust and limits blocks, and the rate rules (A 5.1, 8.2-8.5, 11);

- the room specs as data for C's engine (seam 13), room-trust verification, and the sync filters
  (A 4, 5.2, 5.3, 6.3, 6.4, 6.10);

- references, the verb table and trust classes (A 8.1, 9);

- the composers with their send-side refusals, the secret check and the body forms (A 7, 10);

- the one verdict over an event (A 7.6);

- the question index (A 6.5, 10.2-10.5);

- the body escaper, pinned to one Unicode version (A 13.4);

- the entry lines, the delivery with its caps, order and trailing lines, the MEMBERSHIP notice,
  and the frame text (A 13.1-13.7);

- the shared corpus (A 14), in `testdata/`:

  - `routing/*.json` and `routing-world.json`: the verdict cases;
  - `envelope/*.txt`: the envelope goldens;
  - `escape-classes.json`: the escaper's classes;
  - the pinned sync filters.

  `go test -skip TestEveryScalarValue ./internal/teamchat/proto/ -update` rewrites the corpus.
  Every file in it is also read back and judged on its own.

The package is green on `go test -race`, golangci-lint (0 issues) and `./scripts/qa-changed.bash`.

## Coverage

98.2% of statements (`go test -cover ./internal/teamchat/proto/`), above the 90% floor. The
exhaustive escaper property (`TestEveryScalarValue`, every scalar value) takes about 70 s under
`-race`. It runs the planes in parallel, and planes 0-1 take most of the time. That is accepted
(decision 44).

## The golang.org/x/text vendoring

`golang.org/x/text` v0.39.0 is vendored and allowlisted in `go-deps.allowlist`. It is the
lowest release that fixes GO-2026-5970, which the package reaches through `norm.Form.String`.
v0.43.0 would also bump `golang.org/x/sync`. `norm` ships Unicode tables 15.0.0 (`!go1.27`) and
17.0.0 (`go1.27`). So a Go 1.27 bump fails the Unicode pin test until the corpus is reviewed,
which is what A 13.4 asks for. `go mod verify` is clean, and `vendor/` matches the module cache.

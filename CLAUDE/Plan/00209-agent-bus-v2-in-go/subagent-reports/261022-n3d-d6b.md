## Decisions

- **Pending items count as stored** (the brief says "the ledger"): `single()` already routes an
  id known only to pending to its team, and with `--plain` an item is pending until the next
  `recv`. Answering it from the store keeps the offline rule (it queues while down) and avoids
  a fetch. The lookup relates pending entries too, so the probe's pending `check` is answerable.
- **Every `notice` entry is fetched**, carried or not, since the ledger cannot tell a carried
  item from a lifecycle notice. A lifecycle notice takes a plain reply to its sender; `--verb`
  on it is 10 `re`.
- **A carried item's sender must be current for a plain reply too**, not only for `--verb`:
  replying to an absent member is the same fault (10 `target`, before anything is spent).
- **A carried ping takes `--verb` only when its verb expects an answer**, the ledger rule; the
  carried verbs are v2's (`checkCarried` uses `proto.Verbs`).
- **`--verb` that cannot answer** (`review`, `halt`, ...) is 10 `verb` at once, not left to
  the composer's 10 `schema`, so the message names the four answer verbs.
- **A fetch's outcomes**: Deliver and Record verdicts are items (an `ack` to this member's
  ping is a Record and may be replied to in text); Ignore, Drop, Index, Redact and Fault are 23
  `not-found` ("a verdict that decodes no item"). A refusal the team raised as an exit object
  (`untrusted`, `usage`) is passed through; the request's end is `timed-out`, as in `verify`.

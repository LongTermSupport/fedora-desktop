# n3f spec fix round 1: the fix log

Fixes to `SPEC-n3f.md` for review round 1 (`261023-n3f-spec-review.md`, FIX-FIRST). Every
finding is accepted. The table gives each finding, its fix, and the section and decision rows the
fix touched. No Go.

Seams were checked against the worktree's base and against `feat/bus-v2-n3d` (diffed for
`proto`, `member/state`, `member/records`, `member/engine`) and the handoff that specifies it. One
n3d fact changed several fixes: **n3d-D2 queues every send to a down server, or whose tries all
fail transiently, and answers `sent` with `"queued":true` and exit 0** (the owner's offline rule).
n3f now says once, in its header, that every send here is n3d's.

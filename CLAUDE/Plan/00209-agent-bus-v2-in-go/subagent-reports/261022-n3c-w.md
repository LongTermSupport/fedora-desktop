## Defects found

1. **Owed items lost at a link restart (P1, B44).** `engine.apply` wrote `floor_ms` as the
   batch's high-water. That is the item's own timestamp, so every item still owed at a restart was
   "not newer than its room's floor" and was dropped when restored. B 2.3.2 says the floor is
   "just below the oldest item the store still owes". Fixed in `engine/seam.go` (now `engine/storewrite.go`; `ownedFloors`),
   with `TestPersistFloorsSitBelowTheOldestOwedItem` (red without the fix). Commit `ca8696aef`.
2. **`history`/`verify` against an unreachable homeserver exited 1 `internal`.** B 2.2.4 gives
   20 `unreachable`, 21 `token`, 22 `forbidden` and 14 `server-rate`. `teamsync.verbErr` now maps
   these, because the engine does not import matrix. Test: `TestLN6ServerFailuresAreTheirExits`.
   Commit `ea47b3ee3`.
3. **The e2e harness could SIGKILL its own process group.** When CMD never started, cleanup ran
   `kill(0, SIGKILL)`, which killed `go test`. Cleanup now skips pid 0, `kill` refuses a pid of 0
   or less, and `start` is separate from `launch`.
4. **The hot add was not a defect in the keeper.** `launch` waited for `status --team alpha`,
   which cannot answer while generation 1 holds no seat. The wait is now `seatedIn`, called once
   the seat is expected.
5. **A flake in `keeper/TestChangesWhileRestarting`** under `-shuffle`: it checked beta's seat
   lock before the hot add had taken it. It now waits for the `held` line. Commit `f802b1cb1`.

## Decisions

- **W-D1** (first builder): the keeper passes `--state DIR` to `link`; `link` takes an
  optional `--state`. SPEC-n3 needs a line for it at the merge.
- **W-D2**: the brief's "client verbs report 69 for that team" is wrong. B 2.2.4 keeps 69 for
  "no runtime" (no session, no link). B 2.4.5 says an unreachable homeserver marks the team down
  after 15 s and affects nothing else. So the check asserts the following: `recv` answers at once
  from the store (exit 0); a verb that needs the server exits 20 `unreachable` for that team
  only; `status` shows the team down and the other team up; the other team receives throughout.
- **W-D3**: failpoints are armed once per verb by the test binary. The `failpoint` package is
  unchanged, so the product reads `TEAMCHAT_FAILPOINTS` as before.

## What was built

- `engine/send.go`: `say` and `ping` (SD2), name resolution per team (`addressed`,
  `resolveAll`, `candidates`, `resolve`, `members`), and `send`, steps 3 to 8: the stop, the
  compose, the 16-addressee bound, the forge check, then the charge (gate, entry and caps in one
  store write). `await` answers posted, or queued at the op's deadline. `charge`, the gate
  mapping (`sendGate`, `storeGate`, `keyDigest`), `refusal`, `forgeCheck`, `sendingOf`,
  `storeLookup`, `queuedRecords`.
- `engine/sender.go` (new):
  - `sendLoop` runs once per held team, beside the team goroutine (`start`; `h.cancel` ends
    both).
  - `nextSend` and `mayPost` decide on the state goroutine. They post the oldest send only when
    the recovery is done, the team is up and not stopped, and no hold stands. An untried entry
    is marked tried in a store write before its PUT.
  - `post` re-checks the send against the current roster (or the cached one). A refusal removes
    it, counts it as unsent, logs "was not posted", and answers the waiter with the refusal.
    Then `post` PUTs by txn. On success it calls `RecordOwnSend`, then `recordPosted`.
  - `failed` sorts the failures:
    - 401 or 403: every send an op waits on is removed and answered 21 or 22. Queued sends stay
      (offline rule). The hold lasts until a newer sync with Stopped "".
    - Another non-transient refusal: the send is dropped, counted unsent, and answered
      internal.
    - Anything else: a 30 s hold, which a newer sync also lifts.
  - `RecoverSends` makes one read of main (Backward, limit 100). Every tried entry found there
    (sender self, a message, JSON-equal content, origin at or after `since_ms`) is recorded as
    posted at its origin. Then `recovered` is set and the sender is poked.
- `teams.go`: the new `held` fields are `recovered`, `poke` (cap 1), `waiting` and `unsent`,
  plus `pokeSender`. The LN8 line now reads `..., %d outbox pings and %d queued sends`. A
  removal answers each waiter with not-held.
- `seam.go`: `ledgerRelated` and `pingRelated` are extracted from `view`, which `storeLookup`
  shares. `roomID` takes the main room id. `SetHealth` pokes the sender.
- `status.go`: `Counts.Unsent` is filled, and queued records follow the outbox records.
- `serve.go`: `OpSay` and `OpPing` are served.

## Decisions

The decisions taken by the first builder are listed in the handoff; they still hold. New ones:

- **The hold is built on the state goroutine**, in the same op that records the failure. It
  takes `LastSyncOKMS` at that moment, so a sync that lands between the failure and the hold
  cannot be missed. Without this, SD3-8 raced.
- **`RecordOwnSend` runs right after the PUT, before `recordPosted`.** The resolver then knows
  the send as this member's before the op that made it is answered. A send that is posted but
  not recorded is still the member's own.
- **The unposted log line is written on the goroutine, in the same op as the removal.** A test
  or status that sees the entry gone also sees the line.
- **A store write that fails in the sender** (the tried mark, the removal, the record) logs and
  sets a 30 s hold. It never loops tightly. A record that failed is PUT again by the same txn,
  which the server deduplicates.
- **`RecoverSends` on a team not held returns `team.ErrRemoved`**, like every other team call.
  The old no-op seam test is now `TestN3dTheRecoveryOfATeamNotHeldIsRemoved`.

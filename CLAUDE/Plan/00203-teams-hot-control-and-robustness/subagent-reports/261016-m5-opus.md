## What was built

- **`internal/teams/hot`**
  - `Sessions(ctx)` returns `[]Session`: name, kind, checkout, state, teams (seat, told role),
    `Controllable` and `Unguarded`. It is what `session list` printed, now exported.
  - `SessionLines(rows, home)` lays out the heading and the rows.
  - `OnlyTmuxLine` is the H35 sentence.
  - `session list` is rebuilt on these three, and its output is unchanged (its tests are
    untouched and pass).
  - The `"ccy"` literal became `kindCcy`.
- **`internal/teams/seats`.** `AttachSession(ctx, snap, session)` is factored out of
  `AttachSeat`, so a session is attached by name with a seat attach's checks.
- **`internal/teams/picker`**
  - `sessions.go` holds the Running sessions view and its flows.
  - `Picker.Session` is a `SessionControl` interface: `Sessions` and `Run(ctx, args)`.
    `teams.Wire` sets it to the same `*hot.Hot` that `ccy-teams session` dispatches to.
  - The teams list takes Tab, and its key line says `Tab running sessions`.
  - The help text and `usage.txt` describe the view.
- **Flows.** Each flow asks only for the words, then calls `Hot.Run` with the argv a person
  would type, never with `--yes`. So the command asks its own confirmation and prints its ✓/✗/-
  steps, under the M4 locks and rollback.
  - add: the team, the seat (the lowest free one, or an offered seat no process holds), then
    worker or coordinator. Over a current coordinator, coordinator passes `--demote-current`.
    A worker in a chosen existing seat passes `--role=worker`.
  - remove and move: when the board's roster says the leaving seat is the coordinator, it asks
    "The role goes to no one" or "Hand it to <handle>", giving `--coordinator=demote` or
    `--coordinator=hand-to=<handle>`. With the roster unread, it passes nothing and the command
    asks.
  - role.
  - repair: in the menu only while the session is unguarded.
  - attach.
  - A refused answer (already in it, not installed, homeserver down) is said and asked again,
    3 times at most, per InteractiveScripts.md.

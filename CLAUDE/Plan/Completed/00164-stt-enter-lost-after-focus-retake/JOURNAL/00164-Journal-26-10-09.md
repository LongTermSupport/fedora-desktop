## 15:23 · finding · T2.4   — H2: Enter re-checks focus after a retake

The host debug log weakens H1 and points at H2. In every failing streaming dictation into
a terminal, the panel gave the pinned window focus back ~0.1 s after reporting the loss,
the paste was simulated, and "Enter key sent" followed 1–2 s later: the same paste-to-Enter
gap as a working dictation. So the Enter was not early; nothing checked where focus was
when it went. Leading cause: focus moved away again after the retake and took the Enter.

Change (Task 2.4): after a paste that followed a retake, both recorders ask PasteKey again
just before the Enter, reusing the paste's ask-and-settle loop, and log "still has focus
before the Enter" or "lost focus again after the paste; giving it focus back before the
Enter". A window closed or never given focus back: no Enter and no save, a notification
that stays says the text was pasted but not sent, exit 1. A window that kept focus
throughout is not asked again.

Decision: the 1 s wait before the Enter after a retake stays, and the new ask follows it.
H1 is weakened, not ruled out: the log stamps when the keys were sent, not when the
terminal read the clipboard and drew the text. The wait costs nothing on the common path
(only after a retake), and putting the ask after it means focus is judged at the moment
the Enter goes, not 1 s before it. Host check (Phase 3) should show whether the re-ask
ever finds focus gone; if it never does, H2 is also out and the wait is the remaining lever.

**Ask 1 — a path for a record with no conversation.json.** DECISION: keep the refusal (Decision 10 stands); no fresh-start opt-in, no `--continue` opt-in, no new detached-start command. The two opt-ins reintroduce exactly what Decision 10 (owner's, 2026-10-28) removed, and the gap is a one-time migration: every session launched at <ip>+ records on every SessionStart, so once ask 2 refuses to reboot into it the class is empty; the by-hand route (`ccy --resume` in the folder, picker) already exists. Risk if wrong: one more migration like this one costs an operator a manual resume per session, loudly (ask 3).

**Ask 2 — the cycle must not reboot into a restore it cannot satisfy.** DECISION: hold the reboot and say so (option b), not restart in place. Restart-in-place rides the supervisor's `relaunch.json` through `teams/restart`, which is per-team-seat and cannot cover sessions on no team (most of them), and it would make the cycle mutate live sessions; a pre-warn check is one call and fits the cycle's existing "unwarnable: no reboot, retried next cycle" path (`_warn_and_reboot`, `cycle.py:450`). Risk if wrong: a box with a stubborn unrecorded session never reboots until someone restarts that session, but it is Slacked every cycle and the deploy is not lost.

**Ask 3 — loud failure.** DECISION: make the restore's failures reach `verify-restore`, not `OnFailure=`. The real hole is in `src-go/internal/sessions/restore.go:259-270`: a record that fails is never appended to `r.entries`, so an all-failed restore writes an empty manifest, `verify.go:77-79` says "brought up no sessions; nothing to verify" exit 0, and `cycle.py:486-513` then Slacks `deployed … every restored session is running` — the owner was told success, not nothing. Fix: a failed record becomes a manifest entry with a new `Going` word (e.g. `GoingNotStarted`, detail = the reason), `verify-restore` prints `<name> NOT-STARTED <why>` and exits 1, and the cycle's existing `verify-failed` alert fires with no new sink. Risk if wrong: a record for a deleted checkout now fails verify every boot until removed — intended, Fail Fast.

**Ask 4 — a record asking for a fresh start.** DECISION: intended; document it, no field. A record describes a session that has since run, and its conversation is whatever the hook last recorded, so "launched without `--continue`" says nothing about what should come back; the provisioning-written record it argues from does not exist (no play writes to the registry). Risk if wrong: nil until such a record exists, at which point it is a YAGNI call reopened with a real case.

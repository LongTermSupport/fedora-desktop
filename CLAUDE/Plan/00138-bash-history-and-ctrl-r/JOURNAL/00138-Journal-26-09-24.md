its function-based config because `dev.tty.legacy_tiocsti = 0`. Verified the installed
key-bindings define `__fzf_history__` and bind it with `bind -x` on bash >= 4, so
the merge-then-search wrapper is viable. PROPOSAL.md holds P1–P7 and decisions D1–D4.

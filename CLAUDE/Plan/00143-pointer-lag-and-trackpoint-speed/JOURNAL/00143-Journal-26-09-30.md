## 08:30 · decision · T1.2   — record continuously, correlate after

Third episode, reported after it had ended. Post-hoc triage found nothing, so a long-running capture recorder is now the method.

The owner reported a glitch that had already cleared. A snapshot taken straight
afterwards showed pressure 0, no kernel input/GPU/ACPI lines in 30 minutes, the
dGPU `active` (held by gnome-shell and a Firefox media-decoder process), and the
usual bursts of `Can't update stage views` warnings (up to 310/min). Nothing
localised the episode, and its exact time is unknown.

Decision: episodes are short and reported late, and neither the kernel nor
mutter logs the signals that matter. So post-hoc triage cannot localise them.
Instead, `triage.bash --capture 7200` runs as a bounded background recorder. Its
per-second table already separates the hypotheses. `touchpad/s` staying high
while `i915/s` falls to near 0 means the compositor stopped presenting (H1/H5).
A `resuming` span in `dgpu-transitions.txt` at that moment supports H5.
`touchpad/s` falling means the device path (H2). When the owner reports the next
glitch time, read those rows and the raw libinput events for that window.

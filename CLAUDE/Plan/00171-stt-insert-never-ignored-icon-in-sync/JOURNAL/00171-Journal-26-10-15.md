> **Entry grammar** — append new entries at the BOTTOM; NEVER edit earlier
> entries (corrections are new entries):
>
> ```
> ## HH:MM · category · REF   — optional short title
> ```
>
> - `HH:MM` UTC 24h (the date is in the filename, also UTC); times increase
>   down the file. A file with no sentinel line above predates this system —
>   its times are local, zone unrecorded (legacy; never rewritten).
> - `category` ∈ `action` | `finding` | `decision` | `thought` | `blocker` | `handoff`
> - `REF` = optional task/phase ref (`T1.2`, `P1`) or `—`.
> - Bodies may embed fenced logs/diffs/snippets — no size limit — with a
>   one-line takeaway above the fence.
> - End a working session with a `handoff` entry so the next agent's entry
>   point is the last entry of the newest day-file.

## 21:52 · finding · —

The wsi-stop-grace gate failed once in a full QA run: StopTest.test_stop_drains_the_microphone_and_transcribes_the_last_audio raised FileNotFoundError on events/pw-record.json.

Cause: the fake pw-record in tests/speech_to_text/stt_stubs.py wrote its 32 KiB stop burst and its result file from inside the SIGTERM handler. When the signal lands in a loop write blocked on a full pipe (the server's reader starved on a loaded host), the handler re-enters sys.stdout.buffer, Python raises "reentrant call inside BufferedWriter", the stub exits without writing the result file, the pipe closes, and the session still reaches done. Reproduced deterministically by leaving the pipe unread for 2 s, then sending SIGTERM.

Fix: the handler only sets a flag; the main loop writes the burst and the result file (atomic rename) and exits. Both fake microphones share the prologue. The production stop path (terminate, drain to EOF) is unchanged and still exercised. 120 runs at 40-way parallel under CPU burners and 6 parallel full suites all pass.

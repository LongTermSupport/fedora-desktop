## 19:46 · finding · —   — jq 1.6 in the image misreads UTC times under daylight saving

A second outdated tool in the ccy image showed up the same day: jq 1.6, from Debian bookworm. Its fromdateiso8601 adds an hour to a UTC (`Z`) timestamp whenever the local zone is in daylight saving. Under UTC 1791453600 comes out right; under Europe/London and America/New_York it comes out as 1791457200. Once Plan 00165 gave containers the host's zone, four session-registry tests failed in a ccy session. ccy 3.90.1 works around it by running those two jq calls with TZ=UTC, which stays correct on any jq version. Fedora ships jq 1.7, which does not have the bug, so a Fedora base (D1) removes the cause. jq belongs on the Task 3.9 basics list.

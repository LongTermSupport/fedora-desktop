## Notes (not refutations)

- Claim 4: PLAN writes `Pending.late` (the JSON key); the Go field is `Pending.Late`. Reads fine.
- Claim 15: the handoff says `commit` runs "after the persist succeeds". When the batch is empty
  and the answer is not the first, nothing is persisted and `settle` still runs. A held-only
  answer (the design's case) has an empty batch, so the design's "a failed persist changes nothing"
  does not cover that path: a held entry added to `j.held` would be committed with no persist.
  The design should say whether that is wanted (it likely is, since the floor does not move).
- Claim 17 and 25 are the two claims worth settling before building the forge half.

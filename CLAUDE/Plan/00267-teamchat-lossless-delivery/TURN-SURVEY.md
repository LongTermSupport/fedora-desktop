# Plan 00267 Task 7.8: where else a turn can be saved

The owner (Q19, 26 Nov): a delivery is designed for an agent's turn, not as an API call, and
batch responses are encouraged. This survey walks the agent-side surface of teamchat for further
savings: deliveries, verb outputs, `status`, `recv` and `verify`, and the skill's procedures.
Read in the code on 26 Nov, after Tasks 7.1 to 7.7.

A **turn** is one model turn of the session (a delivery written to its inbox socket starts one).
A **call** is one tool call inside a turn: cheaper than a turn, but each costs a round trip and
the tokens of its output. Savings are given in those units. Cost is the work and the risk.

Already built in Phase 7: the coalesce window and the human settle (7.1, 7.5), notices folded
per member (7.3), the answer-together line in the skill (7.4), no count of entries (7.6), a
delivery in sections, one per kind (7.7).

## Not worth it

- **Acking a PING on delivery.** The link could send `ack` itself once the delivery's proof is
  in. But `ack` means the seat accepted the work, not that it saw the ping; the receipt
  (delivered) already says the second. No saving that keeps the meaning.
- **Dropping verify for a HUMAN request the link fetched itself.** The verify call guards
  against a forged envelope written by anything that holds the socket's token, not against the
  homeserver; the link cannot vouch for what arrives on the socket. It stays.

## The two judgements

- **`<team>: <member> (<c|w>)` with the marks after it is clear.** The colon reads as "on this
  team I am", and two role letters are learned at once. The only ambiguity is `(down)` after
  the member, which could be read as the member being down. The docs say the marks are the
  team's, so it is acceptable.
- **The admin room-state override is the simplest sound choice.** A team-record key would make
  every older member refuse the record and lose trust. A kit file would not reach a ccy image
  or another machine. Admin-only state over the room members already trust copies the topic
  pattern, and an older member never asks for it.

## A success criterion outlived the requirement it mirrored

The review's nit 4 was a defect I introduced. When the owner settled the figure at 600k
and told me to stop hedging about smaller-context models, I removed the "state the
`min(setting, model window)` caveat here too" clause from Task 2.6 — but the matching
success criterion still required it, and was ticked. Two lines describing different
contracts, one of them vouching for a requirement that no longer existed.

No harm reached the docs: `docs/ccy.md` does state the relationship. The point is that
a ticked criterion is a claim, and this one was checking something the plan had stopped
asking for.

Reworded so both lines say the same thing, and so the relationship is framed as what it
actually is — a reason 600k is the right figure on a 1M-context model, not a caveat
against it. That distinction is the whole substance of the owner's correction.

The remaining nit is deliberate and stays: the `min()` relationship and the "/config can
no longer change it" consequence appear in three places — the launcher banner, the plan,
and the user docs. Each has a different reader, and a reader of one is not a reader of the
others. That is duplication with a purpose rather than drift.

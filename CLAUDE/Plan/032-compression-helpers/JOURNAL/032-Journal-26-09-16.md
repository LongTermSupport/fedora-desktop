## 19:42 · finding · T4.1 — the harness found check 5 asserting the wrong tree

Checks 5 and 6 are the ones whose vacuity would matter, so both were lifted out of the gate
and driven against a correct extractor and a deliberately flooding one, with real archives
built by python3's stdlib.

The first run failed the CONTROL. Check 5 asserted `./proj/one.txt`, and the real round trip
produces `./proj/proj/one.txt`: `compress proj` archives a member called `proj`, and
`uncompress` extracts it into a new `./proj/`. That double nesting is Decision 5 of this
plan, accepted deliberately — "1 for safety", no magic flatten. So the check was written to
fail on correct behaviour, and only running it showed that.

Fixed: the members are located by search rather than at a fixed depth, and their CONTENTS
are compared, not just their presence — an extractor producing the right names with empty
files would satisfy a `-f` test.

**The more useful finding is what check 5 cannot do.** Against the flooding extractor it
still passes, and that is structural rather than a bug: the archive `compress` produces has
one top-level member named after the archive, so extracting it into `./proj/` and extracting
it into the cwd yield the SAME tree. No assertion over the result can separate them.

That is precisely why check 6 builds a FLAT zip with the backend directly — a shape the
wrapper cannot produce, and the only one where the tarbomb guarantee is observable. Check 6
does kill the mutant: `a flat zip flooded the working directory: 3 new entries`.

So the two checks now claim what they establish and no more — check 5 the round trip, check
6 the tarbomb protection — and the gate's comments say which is which, instead of both
implying a guarantee only one of them tests.

```
CONTROL — a correct extractor:            PASS=2 FAIL=0
MUTANT  — creates ./bomb/ AND floods:     PASS=1 FAIL=1
```

Check 6's flat-zip fixture is itself asserted flat before use. A fixture that happened to
nest would make the check pass without exercising tarbomb protection at all.

## 19:46 · thought · — — a `032` in a path reads as an octal mode

`chmod +x` on anything under `CLAUDE/Plan/032-compression-helpers/` is refused by the
world-writable-permissions guard: the `032` in the path is parsed as a numeric mode, and
032 has the other-write bit set. Worked around by running the chmod from inside the folder
so the number is not in the command. Noted for the next person who trips over it — the
guard is right about what it sees and wrong about what it is looking at.

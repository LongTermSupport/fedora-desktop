## Findings

1. **Blocking (plan drift).** Decision 2 records that with no key in the agent,
   bash-git-prompt's background fetch writes `Enter passphrase for key:` into the terminal.
   It then accepts this as "upstream behaviour ... not changed here". That contradicts the
   plan's Goal 2 ("never asks for a passphrase") and Success Criterion 2 ("asks for nothing"),
   and no task tracks it. The criterion cannot be ticked as the plan stands.
   - Fix: add a task to stop the prompt's fetch from asking. Examples are a play-set
     `GIT_PROMPT_FETCH_REMOTE_STATUS=0`, or BatchMode for that fetch only.
   - Or reword the goal and criterion with the owner's decision.
2. **Minor false positive.** A successful fetch that matches no refs leaves an empty
   FETCH_HEAD. Two cases: a remote with no branches yet, and a refspec that matches nothing
   (e.g. `refs/tags/*` with no tags). The prompt then shows `[fetch failed or running]` until
   a fetch writes records. I confirmed this against the build. A14 and docs/configuration.md
   should say so.
3. **Minor (noise).** Every successful 5-minute prompt fetch empties FETCH_HEAD. A prompt drawn
   during it shows the mark briefly. This is accepted in Decision 2, but the owner's hand check
   (Task 2.3) should expect it.
4. **Nit.** The triage-probe title "shallow? and remote" prints only the shallow flag. The
   `reach` probe fails its leg on a detached HEAD or a branch with no remote. That failure is
   loud, which is fine.

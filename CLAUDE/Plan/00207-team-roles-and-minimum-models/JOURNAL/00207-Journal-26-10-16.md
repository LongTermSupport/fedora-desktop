Fable re-check: the three original blockers are closed. Two new holes were found in the notary:

- secrets typed into the pane are visible to the tap;
- `notary` is not a reserved localpart.

Resolution:

- **D26 is now a choice.**
  - (a), recommended: the coordinator posts its own labelled `NOTE`. "(coordinator's note) the owner answered Q<n> in the terminal: <paraphrase>" is plainly its claim, and it needs an open `Q<n>`.

- **Why (a):** the owner asked for a record, not proof. A keystroke tap that can see secrets is too much risk for a record with no authority.

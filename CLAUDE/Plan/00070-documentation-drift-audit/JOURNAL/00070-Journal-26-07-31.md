### The distinction the report is built on

Sorting by severity produced a natural split that is worth keeping: **incompleteness misleads by
omission; a wrong command misleads by instruction.** Four docs are in the second class, and a reader
who follows them ends up worse off than if the doc had not existed. That is why they are Phase 2 and
the twelve omissions are Phase 3, rather than everything being ranked by how many files it touches.

Finding 5 is the sharpest: `helpers/CLAUDE.md` teaches a command that prints `OK` while running
nothing — the exact false-green pattern this repo wrote policy about in Plan 00067 and recorded at
`CLAUDE/QA.md:38`. The policy exists, is correct, and a doc two directories away teaches the
violation.

## 11:08 · decision · —   — Launch offer left alone; plain detach; list-clients as the one view

Design decisions.

- **The launch-time offer of `ccy`/`cc` (`ccy_tmux_offer`) gets no take-over choice.** It lists only detached sessions. A project that is open in another terminal on purpose starts a second session there without a question. Listing the open sessions too would put a picker in front of that every time. Instead, its "open in other terminals" lines name `ccy-sessions` Ctrl-T.
- **Plain detach, not `detach-client -P`.** `-P` sends SIGHUP to the other terminal's shell. Freeing the session needs only the client gone, and killing the other login was not asked for.
- **Detach, then wait for list-clients to be empty, then attach, rather than `attach -d`.** `attach -d` collides with the single-attach hook in the same way. Waiting on the hook's own view (list-clients) is the only condition the hook cannot then contradict.
- **Ctrl-T on a detached row attaches, with no question.** It still asks list-clients first, so a suspended holder that the row shows as "detached" gets the take-over question.
- **`ccy_tmux_attach` returns 3 for "kicked, or another terminal on it afterwards".** The take-over passes 3 on, and `ccy-sessions` exits 1. `ccy_tmux_insulate` keeps exiting 0 on 3. There the user may have worked in the session before another terminal took it, and the launch offer should not reappear.

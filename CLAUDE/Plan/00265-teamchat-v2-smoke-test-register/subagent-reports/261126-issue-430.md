# Issue #430: the team chooser offers dead teamchat teams

Register entry: R92 in [REGISTER.md](../REGISTER.md).

## Cause

The bare `--teams` list (`choose-teams`, used by `ccy --teams`, `ccy --resume --teams` and
`cc --teams`) built its teamchat rows from every team with a pool seat in the checkout
(`teams/chooser/teamchat.go` `withTeamchat` over `teams/system/cmd.go` `PoolTeams`). R59's
`Life.Check` drops a team only when this machine's host_vars declare it absent. A team with
no status record and no entry counts as served on another machine, so a retired team whose
server is gone was still offered. The agent-bus rows were judged live (`entries`, the
homeserver unit's state); the teamchat rows never were.

## Class and defence

Class: a team offered to choose whose liveness nobody judged. No analyzer can tell a judged
list from an unjudged one in general (liveness is a run-time fact, and there is no syntactic
shape to match), so no detector rule is written. The defence is the seam: `Teamchat.Answers`
is required, and the list refuses to draw teamchat rows when it is nil (tested). The sweep:
`PoolTeams` has one consumer, the chooser; no other flow lists a checkout's teamchat teams.

## Fix

- `system.Cmd.Answers(ctx, team)` (`teams/system/answers.go`): reads the first pool seat's
  `seat.json`, asks its homeserver for its versions with the pinned CA (`seat.VersionsReach`,
  the take's own reach, no token), bounded by `AnswerLimit` (5 s, a constant) on the injected
  `matrix.Timers`. It returns "" when the bus answers, else why not in words. An unreadable
  `seat.json` or a caller's ended context is an error, never a verdict.
- The chooser asks every team at once (one goroutine each) and leaves out each dead one,
  printing on stderr above the list: `Not offered: team X is dead in this checkout, since its bus does not answer (<why>).` A dead team is no row, so it cannot be chosen. With the dead
  teams gone, a checkout's one live team is taken without a list, as before.
- Wired in `teams/app.go` with `seat.VersionsReach{Clock: matrix.SystemClock()}` and
  `matrix.SystemTimers()`.
- CCY_VERSION 3.124.87 (the claude-yolo front builds from `teams/system`); the image is unchanged.
  `docs/ccy-changelog.md` has the entry, and `meta-deploy.list` describes it on the existing
  play-claude-yolo entry.

## Tests

- `teams/system/answers_test.go`: a live and a dead homeserver, a hanging probe ended at the
  bound on a fake clock, an unreadable seat.json, no seat, a bad name, an ended caller, no probe
  wired.
- `teams/chooser/teamchat_test.go`: a dead team is not a row and is named with why; the one
  live team among dead ones is taken without a list; a probe that cannot judge, or none wired,
  fails the list.
- Red: both test files were written first and failed to compile (no `Answers`, `Reach`, `Timers`).

- A team named alone (`ccy --teams <team>`) is not probed: its seat take already refuses an

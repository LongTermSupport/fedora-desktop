## REFUTED (most consequential first)

1. REGISTER.md R90 part one, "take the seat this checkout last worked in ... and ask only between
   two seats with a conversation". `seatchoice.Pick` (seatchoice.go:100-121) also asks whenever any
   seat is held (a held seat returns false at once, even beside one free used seat), and with no
   seat ever run it takes the lowest free seat, not "the last worked in". Imprecise, not
   misleading about the owner rule. Fix: "takes the one free seat with a conversation here (the
   lowest free seat when none ever ran); asks between two with a conversation, or when a seat is
   held."
2. DESIGN section 3 item 2, "no restored session record already covers (matched by the seats in
   its replay argv, `tmux-session.bash:182`)". The built pass matches in Go: `recordCovers`
   (seatpass.go) reads the record's `--teams` through `discover.RecordTeams`, not the replay argv.
   `tmux-session.bash:182` at HEAD is the comment line of the bash helper
   `ccy_tmux_seats_shared`, which the Go pass does not call. Fix: cite `seatpass.go recordCovers`.

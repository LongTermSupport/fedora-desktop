## 18:31 · decision · — — the seat pass names the seat it starts

Decision (Task 4.2, R89): the restore's seat pass starts each seat from a record naming it,
`--teams <seat>@<team>`, not from the bare `ccy --teams <team>` design section 3 first said.
The sessions the same restore has just started do not hold their seats yet, so a launch by team
could take one of theirs. Naming the seat is deterministic, and the owner never sees the
difference: the record is machine data and every line a person reads is in words. Settled by
the coordinator as an engineering call, not an owner question. Design section 3 is rewritten to
match.

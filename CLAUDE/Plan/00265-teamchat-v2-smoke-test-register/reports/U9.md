# U9 report: an in-thread reply carries its question (R34)

Branch `v2-smoke-u9-thread-question`, from `origin/v2-smoke`.

## Cause

The question an event of the questions room is in was worked out in one place and never asked
for in the others:

- The **delivery** (`engine/deliver.go` `deliveryItems`) built every `deliver.Item` with
  `Question: nil`. Its own comment said so: "the engine holds no question index yet". A human
  post was therefore rendered as `question: none` whatever the index said. `re: -` is correct for
  a thread post, because the thread's fallback reply is not a reply (A 13.2).
- **recv** (`engine/render.go` `itemRecord`) and **history** (`teamsync/verbs.go` `historyOf`)
  built `records.Human` without `question` or `headline`.
- **verify** (`teamsync/verbs.go` `questionOf`) asked the coordinator's index only when the
  index already held the post. Otherwise it fetched the post's thread root or reply target and
  read the question there. That is why verify named Q8 while the envelope said `none`.

The index does attach an in-thread reply in the sync path. The new
`teamsync/thread_question_test.go` pins this for the coordinator's own question: echo, then a
human reply in its thread, gives Q1. So the coordinator's own `reply` and `questions` read the
right attachment for such a post. What the session was shown was wrong.

I could not reproduce item 3 of the entry (`questions` listing an in-thread reply as
`unattached`) offline. A post the index attached to Q<n> is never on its unattached list. The two
posts out of 17 that verify also named `none` are the likely ones listed. If a live check still
shows an in-thread reply in `unattached`, record the post's id. Then the cause is in the index's
fold (an earlier build or catch-up), not in how the question is shown. One suspect, not proven
here: during a catch-up, a human post already in the ledger is judged `seen` and is never
attached. It then becomes unknown to the index (not unattached), and `reply` refuses it.

## The class and its detector

Class: **two paths derive the same fact differently, or one does not derive it at all.** Here the
fact is which question a questions-room event is in, which shows as the envelope's `question:`
line and the records' `question`/`headline`.

Detector: the project rule `project_question_unresolved` (`src-go/qa/rules/questionresolver.go`,
registered in `All()`, catalogued in `CLAUDE/GoProjectRules.md`). Under `internal/teamchat`,
outside tests, it flags:

- a non-empty literal of `records.Human`, `records.Verified` or `deliver.Item` with no `Question`
  key;

- any `Question` or `Headline` value of those types (in a literal, an assignment or a tuple
  assignment) that is not a call of a `team.InQuestion` method.

- **Red, fixture:** `qa/rules/testdata/src/qres/...` gives 9 findings. The resolver's answer, an
  empty literal and a test file are clean (`TestQuestionUnresolved`).

- **Red, real tree** (the driver as `go vet -vettool`, `./internal/teamchat/...`): 6 findings:

  - `engine/deliver.go:206` (`deliveryItems`)
  - `engine/render.go:64` (`itemRecord`)
  - `teamsync/verbs.go:360` (`historyOf`)
  - `teamsync/verbs.go:263`, plus `:268` twice (`verifiedRecord`)

- **Sweep:** those 6 are every instance. All are fixed.

- **Green:** 0 findings from this rule after the fix.

There is one other project-rule finding in the tree, not mine and already on `origin/v2-smoke`:
`engine/status.go:211`, `project_liveness_word` ("online"), from U7's commit db2b31609. The lead
should route it to U7.

## The fix

- `proto.QuestionIndex.QuestionIn(id, h)` is the one resolver:
  - a question root is in itself;
  - a post the index folded is in the question it was attached to, or none;
  - any other post is placed by A 10.4's rules applied to its content now;
  - otherwise the answer is "unknown".
- `team.InQuestion` / `team.InQuestionOf` carry that answer with the headline. They give it in
  the delivery's form (`Entry`) and the records' form (`Name`, `HeadlineName`).
  `team.QuestionNamed` covers a source other than the index: verify's read of a question root,
  for a member with no index.
- `team.Team` gains `QuestionIn(id, h)`. It reads the live index under the room's own lock and
  never blocks.
- Every path now asks it:
  - the delivery (`itemQuestion`);
  - recv;
  - history (questions room);
  - verify, whose relation fetch remains only for a member without the index or a post the index
    cannot place.
- The delivery also resolves a questions reminder (key `<question id>~humans`) to its question.
- Wanted item 2, `reply` answering in the same thread: unchanged. `replyQuestions` already
  answers in Q<n>'s thread from the index's attachment, and for an unattached post in the post's
  own thread. The defect was what the session was shown, which led it to answer elsewhere.

## Live check (coordinator or human)

1. In a team's questions room, the coordinator asks a question: `teamchat ask-human` makes Q<n>.
2. A human opens Q<n>'s thread in the client and replies inside it.
3. In the coordinator's session, the delivery envelope's entry shows
   `room: questions` and `question: Q<n> "<headline>"`, not `question: none`.
4. `teamchat recv` (or `teamchat history --room questions`) shows the same post with
   `"question":"Q<n>"` and the headline. `teamchat verify '<id>' --room questions` agrees.
5. `teamchat reply '<id>'` posts in Q<n>'s thread. `teamchat questions` does not list the post as
   `unattached`.

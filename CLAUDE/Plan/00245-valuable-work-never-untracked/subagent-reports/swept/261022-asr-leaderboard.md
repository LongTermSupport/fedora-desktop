# ASR leaderboard check for tonight's STT tests (report date 2026-10-22)

Target: 4 GB NVIDIA GPU, 93 GB RAM, English long-form dictation (20-60 min), batch.

## Snapshot and its limits

- The live Space (hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard) renders client-side. WebFetch returned no table, so the ranking below comes from a third-party mirror, **snapshot dated 2026-05-06** (benchmarklist.com). It is about 5.5 months old.
- A Hugging Face staffer's post (LinkedIn, via search, secondary) says Zoom Scribe V2 Pro is back at #1 later on. Zoom Scribe is a proprietary cloud API, so it is not testable here.
- The huggingface/open_asr_leaderboard README (current as of 24 Jul 2026) carries no results table. Its trade-off CSV is dated 2026-03-26. Per-family folders include granite, moss-transcribe, omniasr, liteASR and higgs_audio.
- RTFx, params and licence come from each model card (fetched 2026-10-22), not from the snapshot. Where a card did not state a value I say so.
- Leaderboard WER is short-form English (AMI, Earnings22, GigaSpeech, LibriSpeech, SPGISpeech, TED-LIUM, VoxPopuli). It says little about 20-60 min dictation.
- Fit-in-4GB judgements are my estimates from parameter count and precision. No card gave a VRAM figure, and I measured nothing.

## Newer releases (after the snapshot or not on the leaderboard)

- **microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR** (open-sourced 2026-01-21; HF transformers release 2026-03-06). Card text says a 7B model (name "VibeVoice-ASR-7B"; count not stated). MIT repo licence, but the README says research use and no real-world use without more testing. It takes up to 60 min in one pass. It does not fit 4 GB. A CPU BitNet variant (2026-07-23) exists but its accuracy is unknown. A streaming variant followed on 2026-09-03. No WER figures retrieved. Source: https://github.com/microsoft/VibeVoice
- **granite-speech-4.1-2b-plus and -nar** (variants on the 4.1 card). Plus adds speaker labels and word timestamps. NAR is non-autoregressive, for throughput. Not benchmarked here.
- **TII Falcon-ASR** (about 2026-10-07, 1.6B). It targets Arabic, with English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. Weights and licence were not confirmed. Not relevant to English dictation.
- **Nemotron streaming ASR** (NVIDIA). Mentioned on the NVIDIA catalog only. Streaming, English. Details unverified.
- Others seen only as aggregator headlines, not checked: NetEase Confucius4-R2T2 (streaming), Audio8-ASR-Infinite (zh/en streaming), Phonon-2 (Parakeet-based, Mac), Cactus Whistle (edge), inclusionAI Ming-3.0-asr-tiny.
- No Parakeet v4 or new Canary release found (the search found nothing, which is not proof of absence).
- Not checked at all: new Whisper or distil-whisper releases after distil-large-v3.5, and Qwen ASR models newer than Qwen3-ASR.

## Missing from tonight's queue, ranked

1. **nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2** (WER 6.05, English-only, RTFx 3386). It beats v3 (6.32) on English and is the same size and runtime. Fits 4 GB. This is a near-free A/B against your current leader.
2. **CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-03-2026** (WER 5.42, Apache 2.0, 2B). It has the best long-form story (auto-chunking, 55-min example). It does not fit 4 GB in fp16, so test it on CPU (or a quantised build if one exists). It is the best-accuracy candidate.
3. **ibm-granite/granite-speech-4.1-2b** (WER 5.33, #1 in the snapshot, Apache 2.0). It needs a CPU run, with VAD chunking on your side. Granite-4.0-1b-speech (5.52) is a lighter option that might fit with 8-bit.
4. **nvidia/canary-1b-flash** (WER 6.35, 883M, CC-BY-4.0). It is the only extra model here that plausibly fits the GPU, but it needs NeMo chunking and is not clearly better than Parakeet. Low priority.
5. **kyutai/stt-2.6b-en** (WER 6.40, RTFx 88). Its native handling of audio up to about 2 h is attractive, but it is CPU-only and slow. Optional.
6. **microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR** (60 min single pass). It does not fit 4 GB, and the card discourages production use. Only worth a CPU look if 60-min single-pass matters.

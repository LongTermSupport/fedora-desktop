**3.14 decision: settable, not scaled with load.** It was not settable before; only `VMTEST_MEMBER_SSH_TIMEOUT` (the `member-ssh` bound) existed.
- **What changed:** a new `VMTEST_SSH_WAIT_TIMEOUT` sets it, default 600, whole seconds from 1 to 86400. It is refused the same way as `VMTEST_MEMBER_SSH_TIMEOUT`, and the two now share one helper (`secondsSetting`).
- **When it's read:** when the lab is loaded, so a bad value fails before anything boots.
- **What it covers:** every wait for SSH (build, run, `member-up`) and the desktop's wait for its login prompt. The timeout message now names the setting.
- **Why not scale with load:** two readings show no link between load and boot time, and the load average lags and counts processes waiting on disk, so any formula would be a guess no test can check.
- **Why not leave it fixed:** the first attempt under load did time out at 600 s.
- **Why keep 600 as the default:** it is three times the slow 192 s boot, and a guest that never comes up still fails within ten minutes. A caller on a busy host raises it for that call, and the console tail from 3.13 shows whether the guest was still booting or stuck.

The decision and reasons are in SPEC-vmtest E24.

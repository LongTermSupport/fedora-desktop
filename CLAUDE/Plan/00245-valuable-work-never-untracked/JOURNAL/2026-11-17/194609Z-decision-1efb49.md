## 19:46 · decision · — — fieldalignment on: size waste fixed by hand, pointer-ordering a dated exception

Coordinator decision (not the owner's): govet's fieldalignment analyzer is turned on for both Go modules. Its size diagnostic, a struct that padding makes bigger than it need be, now fails the gate. All 103 structs it reported were fixed by hand (101 in the untagged build, two in test files built only with busfailpoints), keeping every field comment: the flags and other small fields moved together, and the few positional literals were reordered with them. Three of them are JSON written to a file: status.json's StatusTeam, the questions index's IndexedQuestion and lxc-online.json's onlineEntry. They were reordered too, which changes their key order. No reader depends on that order. The strict decoders compare JSON values by key (proto.Value.Equal), no spec text makes the order normative, and QX6's byte sums count sizes, which reordering does not change.

The pointer-ordering diagnostic is still excluded. It reports a struct already at its smallest size whose pointers could come first, and saves no memory; the analyzer's own Doc says it very rarely finds a significant problem. The exclusion is an exclusions rule on that message text alone. Its record entry is narrowed to it and keeps its dated review (2026-11-26). Whether it stays excluded for good is the owner's decision at that review. src-go/qa/lintconfig runs the real golangci-lint over a fixture and proves both halves: a size-waste struct is reported, and the pointer-ordering diagnostic is not.

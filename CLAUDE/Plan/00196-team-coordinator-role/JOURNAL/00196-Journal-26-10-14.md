## 13:25 · decision · —   — Design choices for the coordinator role

Design choices made while building, each reversible, listed for the owner.

- The copy is no new wire kind: pingbus adds the coordinator to `to` and `m.mentions` of a
  worker's text or ping that names a human. Receivers need no new rule; the receiver derives
  "on copy" (it is the coordinator and humans are among the addressees) and says so in a new
  last field `copy` of the PING and PEER lines (`copy:alice` or `-`). Fields are only appended,
  so older readers of the lines keep working. Protocol stays version 1.
- Older pingbus builds read a team record that says `coordinator` as untrusted, so every member
  takes the new pingbus before the team names a coordinator (the same rule `org` and `exclude`
  set). Writers write `coordinator` from now on; `orchestrator` reads as coordinator.
- The one-coordinator limit is held by the writers (agent-bus add-member and set-role, naming the
  holder). The receiver does not refuse a record listing several (an old record may): it treats
  each as a coordinator.
- `--to-orchestrator` is renamed, no alias: only the pingbus skill and its tests used it.
- `--to` and `--to-coordinator` combine on both `send` and `say` (the owner said "and/or").
- A new seat has no member until the launch makes it, so the role question is asked for seats
  that exist; a new seat joins as a worker and is said so.
- The seat chooser was ported from bash to Go (`ccy-teams choose-seat`) so the role question
  has a home and the launcher and its seats library shrink rather than grow.

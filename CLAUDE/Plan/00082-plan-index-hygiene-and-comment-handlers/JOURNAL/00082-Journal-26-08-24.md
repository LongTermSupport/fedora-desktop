## The backlog behind the backlog

Trimming the 39 rows was the job as briefed. Re-running `plan-qa --sweep` afterwards found
**27 blocks**, and 25 of them were `status-enum-and-date`: plan headers decorated with a
status emoji (`🔄 In Progress`, `🟢 Complete`) or carrying a paragraph of prose after the
token.

The interesting part is what that was hiding. A header the checker cannot parse is a plan
the *other* checks cannot evaluate. Normalising the 25 headers — emoji stripped, trailing
prose relocated, nothing deleted — immediately surfaced findings that had always been true
and never reported:

- two plans with a **terminal status sitting in the active root** (`00050-fedora-44-tracking`,
  `025-ccy-spring-cleaning`), now `git mv`'d into `Completed/` with their README rows;
- **eleven** plans marked In Progress with 67–192 days of no commits (`dormant-honesty`).
  Left alone deliberately: marking someone's plan Dormant is a claim about intent, and the
  check's own remedy asks for a parenthetical naming what it is blocked on — which cannot
  be invented. Handed to the owner.

Also fixed while in there: `CLAUDE/Plan/README.md` advertised `validate_plan_number`, a
handler removed in daemon 3.53.0.

## Two things that proved themselves by firing on me

**1. `header-body-coherence` on 00065 is a false positive**, and the proof arrived by
accident. The trigger is the literal success banner `run.bash` prints — a two-word all-caps
string — which that plan quotes in an *unticked* task and an *unticked* success criterion.
It is naming its acceptance signal, not claiming completion. Rewording the surrounding
sentence did not clear it. Then the first draft of Decision 3 in this plan quoted the banner
to explain the problem, and **this** plan was blocked by the same check. Left unfixed and
documented; `legacy_plan_allowlist` would silence it but downgrades every finding for that
plan, which trades one false positive for real blindness.

**2. `lint_on_edit` and the semgrep gate both caught this plan's own triage script.**
`lint_on_edit` — newly effective since 3.52.0 resolves linters from the project venv —
denied an edit over a shellcheck `info` finding (`tr 'A-Z' 'a-z'`). Then `qa-all.bash`
failed on two `|| echo "(none)"` fallbacks in the P2 probe: grep's no-match status
swallowed by a fallback, inside a script written to measure exactly that defect class in
other people's code. Both fixed; QA green at 535 files.

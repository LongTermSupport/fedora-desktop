## 11:23 · decision · —   — where the pause lives, and the defaults

Design decisions, defaulted without the owner (each recorded in PLAN.md):

- The pause lives in a separate Go command, `wsi-pause-media`, started and released by the
  panel (`panelSession.js` through `mediaHold.js`), not inside `wsi`/`wsi-stream`: Escape in
  batch mode SIGKILLs the recorder's process group, so a recorder could never resume what it
  paused, while the panel sees every recording's start and end.
- D-Bus through `busctl --user --json=short` (systemd, on every host; JSON rather than gdbus's
  GVariant text). playerctl is installed by no play and is not needed. `busctl` was owned by
  `internal/systemd` in the Go project rules table; `internal/wsimedia` is added as its second
  owner (a different job: MPRIS on the session bus).
- Resume when the microphone closes (TRANSCRIBING and later), not when the recorder exits, so
  the music is back while transcribing and Claude post-processing run.
- Players are tracked by unique name: one replaced under the same well-known name is not
  resumed. CanPause false is never paused.
- A hold failure is reported with the panel's error notification but does not stop the
  dictation.
- The extension's metadata.json version is left at 1: no rule requires a bump and it has never
  been bumped for a feature.

## 13:30 · decision · —

Phase 4 (the owner asked for the volume lowered to a third instead of a pause): decisions, red and green.

Decisions (each in PLAN.md "Decisions (Phase 4)"):

- Per-stream volume (PulseAudio sink input on PipeWire's pulse server), not the MPRIS Volume
  property (optional; Firefox and Chromium do not implement it) and not the output volume (it
  would quieten the system's own sounds and every other app, and its restore would undo a
  volume key pressed meanwhile).
- pactl --format=json (pulseaudio-utils, now named in the play with pipewire-pulseaudio): JSON
  rather than wpctl's text or pw-dump's whole graph on a cubic scale; raw integer volumes set
  channel by channel, so the restore is exact and a balance kept.
- Player to stream: uncorked sink inputs whose application.process.id is the player's process
  (GetConnectionUnixProcessID) or a descendant (via /proc/<pid>/stat), else those whose
  application.process.binary or application.name is the first word of the MPRIS name. No
  stream found: paused as before. pactl unreadable: every playing player paused, exit 1.
- Restore only a stream with the same index and process whose volume is within 1% (655/65536)
  of the value set (the server keeps float volumes); otherwise the owner changed it and it is
  left; a gone stream is skipped.
- Renamed wsi-pause-media to wsi-lower-media-volume and the key to
  lower-media-volume-while-recording (neither ever deployed: the play was still queued); the
  play removes the old binary.

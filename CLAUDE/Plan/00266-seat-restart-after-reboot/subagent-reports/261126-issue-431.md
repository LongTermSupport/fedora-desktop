# Issue #431: a renamed session's restore never starts fresh silently

## The defect

`conversation.StartPlan` finds a session's conversation by two keys: the session's name, read
on the system its teams are on now (`Locate`), then its seats' records (`bySeats`). A session
renamed from `<old>` to `<new>` whose teams moved to teamchat (v2) has no v2 record under
`<new>` and no seat record. Its conversation is in the agent-bus (v1) record
`.claude/ccy/pingbus/sessions/<old>/conversation.json`. Both lookups miss, the miss reads as
"no conversation is recorded", and a record marked `fresh-if-no-conversation` is started fresh,
with nothing said.

**The class:** the restore looks a conversation up by a key (the session name, the system its
teams are on) that a rename or a move to teamchat changes. A miss by those keys was taken to
mean that nothing is recorded.

## The fix

`internal/conversation/elsewhere.go`:

- **When it runs.** StartPlan calls `recordedElsewhere` whenever the record's own lookup and
  its seats find nothing.
- **What it scans.** Every v1 session record in the checkout, read as a restore reads it.
  - It skips names that another registry record owns (a conversation of `ccy-app-2` is that
    session's).
  - It skips the path the record's own lookup already read.
  - It skips records whose conversation has no transcript: there is nothing to resume.
- **What counts.** A readable record with a transcript, or an unreadable record (it might be
  this session's).
- **What happens.** Any such record means the start is refused, marked or not (`elsewhereError`).
  The refusal names:
  - each record found (name, conversation id, path);
  - the repair: `ccy-teams seats conversation <seats> --set <id>` for a seated record, or
    `<prefix> --resume <id> in <dir>` for an unseated one;
  - how to start fresh on purpose.

`restore`, `restore --check`, `declare` and the team restart all ask StartPlan, so the check
reports `UNRESTORABLE no-conversation` before a reboot.

**Should fresh-if-no-conversation apply to a session that has run before?** No. It now applies
only when no conversation is recorded for the checkout under any unowned name.

**Not searched:** a v2 session's own state under another name. A clean end carries it into the
seat records, which `bySeats` reads. There is no "rename" verb to carry the record: a new name
comes from a declare or a seat-named launch. The refusal's `--set` repair writes the seat
record, and from then on that record is what the restore follows.

## Review fixes

The review of `cf98ce8ce` returned FIX-BEFORE-MERGE. Each finding and what answers it:

1. **Records nothing ever cleared.** A v1 record whose session had gone (forgotten, or ended)
   refused every later fresh start in its checkout.
   - `conversation.Retire(checkout, name)` moves that `conversation.json` aside, to
     `conversation.retired.json` beside it, which no restore reads; the id stays for a person.
     It goes through an `os.Root` on the checkout, and refuses a link on the way or at the file.
   - `ccy-sessions forget NAME` retires the forgotten session's own record first
     (`conversation.RetireOwn`), so a refused retire keeps the registry record.
   - The explicit verb is `ccy-sessions retire-conversation [--] CHECKOUT NAME`. It is refused
     when a registry record answers to NAME (forget it instead), when a live session has it,
     when the live list cannot be read, and when there is nothing to retire.
   - The refusal names the verb, one line per record found. The #431 case is still refused,
     marked or not, and nothing starts fresh until a person retires the record.
   - Not covered: a session ended by `/exit` (the trampoline, which is bash) or by teamchat's
     clean end leaves its own v1 record. The refusal names the verb for that case too.
2. **Fresh on purpose, headless.** The refusal now gives commands, not "start it by hand":
   - marked: retire each record, then `ccy-sessions restore NAME` starts it fresh;
   - unmarked ccy: retire, then `ccy-sessions declare --name NAME --fresh-if-no-conversation
     --replace DIR -- <its compose answer and arguments>`, then the restore;
   - cc (declare writes no cc record): retire, then a new conversation by hand.
   The refused record is no longer called stale (`conversation.ElsewhereError`, so `staleNote`
   says nothing).
3. **Docs.** `docs/ccy.md` (the fresh-if-no-conversation paragraph and a new one) and
   `sessions/usage.txt` (forget, the new verb, the fresh paragraph) now say that a marked record
   with no conversation of its own and none its seats name starts fresh, except beside such a
   record.
4. **Fixture module.** `fix/go.mod` requires the stub modules at `src-go/go.mod`'s versions
   (`charm.land/bubbles/v2 v2.2.1`, `charm.land/bubbletea/v2 v2.1.0`, `golang.org/x/sys
   v0.47.0`) and replaces each with its stub under `testdata/src`, which gets a `go.mod` of its

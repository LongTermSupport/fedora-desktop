## Job 2: the cause

The test is `src-go/internal/ui/drain_pty_test.go`. It runs the real gum 0.17 in a
pseudo-terminal with TERM tmux-256color, under a real `ttyecho.New` hold and `execx.OS`, inside
`Gum.Destructive()`. It writes Esc on the confirm, then a Down as a separate write.

- **(a) No hold on this path: refuted.** A recording `Modes` saw one flush before each of the two
  questions.
- **(b) Ordering: confirmed by timing.**
  - The confirm exits about 3 ms after the Esc, and the flush runs straight away.
  - `gum choose` starts at about Esc+4 ms and draws at about Esc+45 ms.
  - So a Down sent 5 to 40 ms after the Esc arrives after the flush but before the screen draws.
    gum reads its input from the moment it starts, so it takes the key.
- **Red:** 4 of the 5 gaps carried the key. The 1 ms gap was still caught by the flush here. On
  the host each `tmux send-keys` starts a process, so its "1 ms" arrives later.

Why 12.3's pty test passed while the host failed: it wrote the keys before calling the drain,
and it ran no real program. It only proved that tcflush discards input that is already queued.

## The fix

`ttyecho.Hold.Settle` is a new seam. Its real form is `time.Sleep(settleTime)` with
`settleTime` = 150 ms. A screen marked DrainInput now waits that long, then flushes, then runs.
The wait happens without the lock, so SIGTERM can still put the terminal mode back during it.
The package doc says this, and also names what is left (below).

**Green:**

- All 5 gaps stay on "Leave it running".
- The type-ahead half still works: answer y, then Down 20 ms later, outside a destructive flow,
  and the cursor moves.
- New ttyecho tests:
  - the settle comes before the one flush, and the screen runs after it;
  - the real settle is 150 ms, checked in a synctest bubble;
  - on a real pty, a key that arrives during the settle is discarded.

**Not covered:**

- A key pressed after the 150 ms but before gum draws (gum's own start, about 40 ms) still
  reaches it. Closing that would need control over the screen's input, which gum does not give.
- Every draining screen now waits 150 ms. That is the screen after a leave, and every question
  of `remove`, `restore` and End.

## 02:10 · finding · Task 12.6   — The drain pty test raced its own cleanup on a failure

Task 12.6, second defect: a data race in the drain pty test, found by the coordinator's qa-all
(go-qa runs `-race`).

On a failure, gumPty's cleanup closed the pty while the gum goroutine still held the terminal.
The goroutine had read the fd in `os.StartProcess`, and nothing ordered that before the close.
A probe that drives the failure path (Esc and Down in one write) went red under `-race` with
DATA RACE.

Fix: takeDownSteps' cleanup waits for the goroutine. t.Context is cancelled first, which ends
gum, and cleanups run last in, first out, so the wait comes before the close. The probe then
failed 3 of 3 with its own message only, and no race.

With `-race`, under load: 0 of 50 failed (1 ms) and 0 of 150 (all gaps). Beside a parallel
`go test -race ./...`: 0 of 100. F44's test failed 2 of 50, with the race. Without the settle,
under `-race`: 10 of 10 runs red, idle and loaded.

## 21:23 · decision · —   — headless replay of no saved network joins podman

The host agent noted that any real headless relaunch replaying a Quick Launch record with no saved network also landed on pasta (--no-network) and so could never ccy --connect, policy or not.

The line came from Plan 00161 U31 (headless Quick Launch) and existed only to keep a headless launch out of the network picker. An empty LAST_NETWORK is also what a launch onto the default podman network saves, and an interactive yes to the same record joins podman. So the headless replay now sets CCY_NO_PROJECT_NETWORK=true, which takes the restart's "no project network" branch (no detection, no offers) and lands on podman, as the interactive launch did. Tests seen red first: scripts/test-ccy-teams.bash (2 cases) and scripts/test-ccy-restart-request.bash (the pinned branch, 1 case). Both are green after the change, and the difftest parity passes.

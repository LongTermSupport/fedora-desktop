## 20:57 · finding · —   — acceptance run 1: three check-design fixes

Acceptance run 1 was rejected on blocked, connect and bus; each was the check's design, not the policy.

- blocked: host-other was the first host address off the outbound interface, which the unpoliced control could not reach either (curl 7): pasta copies that address into the rootless namespace. The check now probes every such address from the control and puts only those it reaches to the session; with none it exits 2, and acceptance.bash now carries a check's exit 2 to its own COULD NOT ESTABLISH.
- connect: np232c's Quick Launch record had an empty LAST_NETWORK, and a headless replay of that is --no-network, so it ran on pasta and podman network connect refused it. np232c now replays LAST_NETWORK="podman", the network a plain launch joins. ccy --connect to a pasta session printed the engine's error and called it unusual; CCY 3.101.1 says the session was started without a network, cannot join one, and to relaunch with --network.
- bus: pingbus inbox lists only pending items and never their text, so the nonce could not have matched, and the woken receiver consumed the delivery anyway. The check now reads the receiver's pingbus history for a received PEER item with the event id say printed.
- The seated throwaways take turns of their own on the membership notice and the probes; harmless, recorded as F44.

Each fix was seen red first: test-acceptance-checks.bash (fakes for podman, ip and sleep) failed 10 cases against the old checks, the three host failures among them; scripts/test-ccy-network-disconnect.bash failed 8 new cases against the old connect. Both are green after the fixes.

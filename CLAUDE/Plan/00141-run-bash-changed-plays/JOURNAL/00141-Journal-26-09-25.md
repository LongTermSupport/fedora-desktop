## 20:08 · finding · T1.1   — play-basic-configs read as depending on all of files/

That play copied its three bash tweak files in a loop over `{{ root_dir }}/files{{ item }}`.
The mapper can only take the prefix `files/`, so any change under `files/` would have
listed the play as changed. The fix split the loop into three copy tasks, each with a full
`src`, and the play now has no prefixes and nothing unresolved. It passes a syntax check.

## 20:08 · action · T2.1   — run.bash --changed (1.28.0)

The flag is refused with a playbook path, `--optional-only`, `--headless`, or a streamed
run.bash. It lists the plays, confirms once (default no), takes one play lock, and runs
each play through `run_playbook_with_issue_option`. At the first failure it stops, names
the plays not yet run, and exits with that play's status.

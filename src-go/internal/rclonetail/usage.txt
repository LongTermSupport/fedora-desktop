Usage: rclone-tail [--interval N] [--once] [MOUNT_NAME]

Watch the rclone mounts' uploads live: queue, uploads in flight, transfer rate and ETA.

  MOUNT_NAME      watch only the mounts of this remote (e.g. 'lib'); default: every mount
  -i, --interval  seconds between ticks, 1 to 86400 (default: 5)
  -o, --once      print one tick and exit, with no cache snapshot
  -h, --help      show this help

Each tick asks every mount's --rc endpoint for vfs/stats (the upload queue) and core/stats
(bytes, transfers, ETA). A mount with nothing queued, nothing uploading and no bytes moving is
'idle', so active mounts stand out (pipe through `grep -v idle` to see only those). A mount
started without --rc is listed as '(no --rc; cannot query)'.

Without --once the rclone-cache-status summary is printed first, and again when Ctrl-C (or
SIGTERM) ends the watch.

Exit status: 0 the watch ended with Ctrl-C, or the --once tick was written; 1 no rclone mount
(matching MOUNT_NAME) was found, the mounts could not be listed, the report could not be
written, or rclone is not installed; 64 a usage error.

Installed by play-rclone.yml.

ccy-teams — see the agent teams on this machine and look after them: each team's
homeserver, its members, the people in its room, and the ccy sessions in its seats. It is
to teams what ccy-sessions is to sessions (Plan 00161, Plan 00177; docs/agent-bus.md,
"Getting started").

A team is declared in the untracked host_vars localhost.yml (agent_bus_teams) and made by
play-agent-bus.yml. A ccy session joins one with `ccy --teams <seat>@<team>`, an LXC
container's agent through agent_bus_lxc_members. ccy-teams holds no team logic of its own:
it acts through agent-bus, agent-bus-install, ccy and the session registry. It edits the
host_vars only to add a team (create) or to declare one absent (remove), and every such
change is applied at once: it runs play-agent-bus.yml for that team alone (its own
shebang, through run.bash's single-play mode), so no team is left declared but not built.

Each team is a row (its homeserver up, down or not installed yet, its port, and APPLIED:
whether what runs is what the host_vars declare, DESIGN.md D65), followed by its members:
  ccy                    a seat of a ccy checkout, with the ccy session holding it and
                         whether that session is started again after a reboot
  lxc, host, docker, vm  a member of that type; a ccy member whose checkout is not
                         known here (no session or registry record names it) is "ccy"
                         with no checkout
  human                  a person in the team room: in it or not, and how many Element
                         sessions they are signed in with
Keys, shown in the picker's header too (? there shows them all, explained):
  Enter    on a seat: attach its session (as ccy-sessions does); on a person: set a new
           Element password and show the login; on a team: this help
  Ctrl-N   start a new ccy session in a free or new seat of the row's team, here
  Ctrl-P   a new Element password for the person on the row
  Ctrl-E   open the team in Element (setting its profile up first, when it has none)
  Ctrl-U   update every team that is out of date or not built (never one to take down)
  Ctrl-R   turn the seat's session's restore after a reboot off or on
  Ctrl-X   remove a seat, park a member, or take a team down (each after a yes/no)
  Esc, q   leave
With no team on this machine, the picker offers to create one; with a team out of date, it
offers to update it first; with a team still holding the old default path_prefixes, it
offers once to remove them. Run in a checkout of a team's repositories, with no session of
that checkout in the team, it offers to start one there (ccy --teams <team>); run in a
GitHub checkout no team covers, it says so and offers to create a team for it.

Reading the members needs root (the team's admin token is root's). play-agent-bus.yml
installs a sudoers rule that lets you run exactly `agent-bus list` and `agent-bus human
devices` without a password; where that rule is not installed yet and sudo needs a
password, the picker says why before sudo asks, and stops, saying so, when sudo is
refused. --list never asks: without sudo it shows the members as "need sudo", and, as with
any part of the table it could not read, it still prints the rest but exits 1.

Usage:
  ccy-teams                        the picker (needs a terminal)
  ccy-teams -l|--list              print the same table, with a heading, and exit
                                   (colour only on a terminal, and never with NO_COLOR)
  ccy-teams create [--org ORG | --repo OWNER/REPO...] [--team T] [--human NAME]...
                   [--allow-from CIDR]... [--yes]
                                   add a team to agent_bus_teams in the host_vars, with
                                   its Element profile in agent_bus_element_teams, and
                                   install both. On a terminal it asks (with gum) for what
                                   the flags leave out: what the team works on (one
                                   repository, the default in a checkout, which it names;
                                   everything one GitHub owner owns; or several
                                   repositories of any owners, each one you can push to),
                                   all on any branch; the name (the repository's or the
                                   owner's, by default); the people in the team room (you,
                                   by default); other machines' subnets. It shows a
                                   summary and asks once, then writes the entries and runs
                                   play-agent-bus.yml and play-agent-bus-element.yml, and
                                   says it is done only when both are applied. Then it
                                   offers each person's sign-in, opens Element, and, in a
                                   checkout of the team's, starts a ccy session there.
                                   --yes asks and offers nothing: give --org or --repo,
                                   --team and --human. The port is chosen for you
  ccy-teams apply <team> | --all   run play-agent-bus.yml for the team (or every team
                                   out of date or not built), and play-agent-bus-element.yml
                                   for those with an Element profile, then check each now
                                   matches its declaration. --all never takes a team
                                   down: a team declared absent, or built but no longer
                                   declared, needs your decision (ccy-teams remove)
  ccy-teams remove <team>          take a team down, on a terminal only: it says what is
                                   lost, offers a backup first, and keeps the team's data
                                   unless you choose to delete it and type its name
  ccy-teams backup <team>          back the team's homeserver up now
  ccy-teams restore <team> [<n>] [--yes]
                                   restore backup <n> after a yes/no (--yes: none);
                                   without <n>, choose one from the list
  ccy-teams -h|--help              this text

Backups stay on this machine (docs/agent-bus.md, "Backups are the root of trust").
The APPLIED column, from `declare.py status` against the installer's stamps (readable
without sudo): up to date, out of date (config, software or both), not built, removed,
or needs your decision (declared absent but still built, or built but not declared).
Exit 64 for a usage mistake, 130 when cancelled, 1 for a refusal or a failure.

Runs on the HOST, as the desktop user.

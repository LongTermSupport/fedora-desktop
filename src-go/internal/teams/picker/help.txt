Each team is a bold row: its homeserver (up, down, or not installed yet), its port, and
APPLIED, whether it runs what the host_vars declare:
  up to date          as declared
  out of date: ...    the declaration (config), the bus software, or both changed since;
                      Ctrl-U (or ccy-teams apply) brings it up to date
  not built           declared, never installed: Ctrl-U builds it
  decide: absent      declared absent but still running; decide: undeclared, running but
                      no longer declared. Never updated in bulk: Ctrl-X on the team takes
                      it down (its data kept unless you delete it)
Under it, its members and people:
  ccy      a seat of a ccy checkout: the session in it, and RESTORE, whether that
           session is started again after a reboot (yes or no)
  lxc ...  another member: active, or parked (its token revoked)
  human    a person in the team room: in room, invited or not in room, and how
           many Element sessions they are signed in with

Keys:
  Enter    a menu of everything that can be done to the row, in words, each with its
           shortcut beside it (Esc goes back to the list). The keys below are those
           shortcuts, and each works only on a row whose menu has it: nothing needs a key
  Ctrl-N   start a ccy session in a free or new seat of the team, in this folder
  Ctrl-P   a new Element password for the person on the row (shown once). If they are
           signed in to Element it asks first, saying how many sessions it signs out
  Ctrl-E   open the team in Element; it starts apart, and you stay on the list. Each
           team is its own Element window (each is its own Matrix server); Enter on a
           team row has Open every team in Element, a window each
  Ctrl-U   on a team row: update every team out of date or not built (never takes one
           down)
  Ctrl-R   restore after a reboot, on or off, for the seat's session
  Ctrl-X   remove a seat, park a member, or take a team down (after a yes/no; a
           backup first, and the data kept unless you type the team's name)
  Tab      the last row, Running sessions (below)
  ?        this help          Esc or q   leave

Running sessions, the last row of the teams (or Tab), lists every session ccy's tmux started
(the ones ccy-sessions lists): its checkout, its teams (seat, and the role it was told) and
its state. Its teams can be changed while it runs; nothing restarts, except a session started
on no team, which relaunches once on its first join. A ccy run in a plain terminal is not
listed and cannot be changed. Enter on a session lists what can be done to it:
  Add to a team             the team, the seat (the lowest free one, or one you pick)
                            and the role
  Remove from <team>        for the team's coordinator, it asks whether the role goes to
                            no one or to another member
  Change role in <team>     worker or coordinator; on a team with none the choice is
                            Make it the coordinator, and on a team that has one it is
                            Replace the coordinator, which asks again (no first)
  Move to another team      the team it joins (with several teams: Move from <team>)
  Attach                    this terminal joins the session
  Repair                    only when its seat keeper has stopped (unguarded)
"What this list shows" (or ?) is this help; "Back to the teams" (or Esc) goes back.
Each change is `ccy-teams session add|remove|move|role|repair`, run for you: it asks
before it changes anything and prints each step (✓ done, ✗ failed, - already so).

Add an LXC container's agent to <team> is in the menu of every row (Enter; it has no key).
It lists the host's running LXC containers and asks for the container, its user, the
checkout inside it and the role (the handle comes from the checkout), shows a summary, writes the
agent_bus_lxc_members entry for you and applies the team. It is the same as
`ccy-teams lxc-add <team>`.

A seat marked "(coordinator)" is its team's coordinator: the agent that takes the humans'
requests, dispatches them to the workers and is copied on what workers tell humans. A team
has one. Enter on a seat has Make it the coordinator when the team has none, Stop being
coordinator on the coordinator's seat, or, last, Replace the coordinator when another seat
has the role: that takeover names the current coordinator and whether it runs, says it loses
the role, and starts on no. Each asks first and needs sudo.

At its start ccy-teams offers to describe a team that has no description, and to lift an old
folder limit: now, not now (asked again next time; Esc is the same), or never. A team you
said never to has "Ask me to describe <team> again" (or "Ask me about <team>'s limit again")
in its row's menu.

Colours: green is working, yellow wants a look, red is not working.
"need sudo": reading the members needs root; play-agent-bus.yml installs a rule that
lets ccy-teams read them without a password.

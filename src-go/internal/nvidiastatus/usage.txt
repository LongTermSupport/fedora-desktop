Usage: nvidia-status [--check]

Reports whether the NVIDIA driver installed by play-nvidia.yml is built, loaded and working:
Secure Boot and the akmods MOK key, the packages, the kernel modules, nvidia-smi, OpenGL,
Vulkan, VA-API, and the kernel's NVIDIA errors of this boot. It only reads; nothing is changed.

  --check     print nothing; the exit status alone is the verdict
  -h, --help  show this help

Exit status: 0 the driver is complete and functional; 1 it is not (the summary says what to
do), or the report could not be written; 64 a bad command line.
